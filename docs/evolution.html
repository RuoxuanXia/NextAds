NextAds paradigm

Closed-loop Creative Refinement

NextAds is a generative, closed-loop paradigm for video creative optimization. Reflector converts pre-deployment assessments and post-deployment interaction signals into actionable update directives.

Pre-deployment verificationVerifier provides explicit diagnostic evidence for targeted refinement before deployment.
Post-deployment user feedbackOnline signals provide implicit preference and performance evidence for subsequent creatives.

Post-deployment evolution

Creative Evolution Across Impressions

NextAds enables serving-time, closed-loop creative evolution at two levels: refining individual creatives based on online user feedback, and coordinating them over longer horizons through progressive storytelling.

Creative level

Iterative Refinement Across Impressions

Across repeated impressions, NextAds can adapt subsequent creatives to newly observed user feedback, evolving serving contexts, and repeated-exposure effects.

Trajectory level

Progressive Storytelling

Later impressions build on earlier ones, with each impression serving a distinct role in progressively developing the user–product narrative.
